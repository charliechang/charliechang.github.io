Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

Friday is a private AI assistant used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles Google user data.

Data accessed

With the account holder's consent, Friday accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Tasks and Contacts for its own dedicated Google account, and items explicitly shared with that account.

How data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks requested by the owner, such as summarizing messages, drafting replies for the owner's review, and reading calendar events or shared documents. Friday does not send email on its own.

Storage and sharing

Data is processed on a machine controlled by the owner. It is not sold, not shared with third parties for advertising, and not used to train models by the owner. Requests are processed by the AI model provider the owner uses, under that provider's terms.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Friday's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
